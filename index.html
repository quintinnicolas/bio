Nicolas Quintin co-founder of Octave.energy

Current work

I'm a co-founder and head of EMS at Octave.energy, a Belgian cleantech company specializing in Energy Management Systems (EMS) and battery energy storage solutions for businesses. Octave has sold over 200 MWh of battery storage capacity and operates the largest fleet of behind-the-meter batteries in Belgium.

Our mission is to accelerate the energy transition with scalable, cost-effective solutions that meet the growing need for flexibility in the energy system.

Our Energy Management System (EMS) offers a comprehensive range of control and optimization applications for batteries and other flexible energy assets, including solar PV and EV charging infrastructure.

Feel free to reach out if you're interested in what we do. We're hiring!

Previous work

Before Octave, I built my career at the intersection of energy and data. At Gogoro in Taipei, I worked as a battery data engineer, improving charging algorithms of their battery-swapping network to extend battery lifespan. Earlier, I modelled European power markets at ENGIE Impact, traded electricity on short-term power markets at Lampiris (TotalEnergies), and started out as an energy consultant at Sia Partners.

Education

I hold a dual Master's degree in electromechanical engineering from UCLouvain and in energy from KU Leuven. My thesis, written with Belgium's transmission system operator Elia, assessed non-linear frequency containment reserves in the power system.
